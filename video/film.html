How it works
Two speeds of thinking.

System 1 · Jev

Decides. Never writes.

Every step it sees the screen as text and picks one action from a closed list. per decision, each. No memory.

System 2 · Fable 5.1

Reviews. Rewrites.

After every 10 invoices it reads the full decision log, finds where Jev wasted steps, and rewrites the playbook. It never touches the screen.
